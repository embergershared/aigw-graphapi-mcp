# Support

This is a personal project, shared as is under the [MIT license](../LICENSE). It is not an
official Microsoft product, and Microsoft does not support it.

- **Questions and bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/__REPO__/issues) with what you tried, what you
  expected, and what happened. Leave out customer names, tenant or subscription IDs, secrets,
  and real data.
- **Security problems:** see [SECURITY.md](../.github/SECURITY.md); never in a public issue.
- **Microsoft products and Azure services:** use their official support channels.

Answers are best effort, with no response-time commitment.
